Integrity

Integrity means believing in being honest and doing the right thing, even when no one is watching. Integrity guides my choices and helps me build trust with others. I feel that when I meet people, one of the most important traits I would like them to have is integrity. Integrity to me is the root of a good trait, for example, loyalty and honesty.

Loyalty

Loyalty is huge for me. I need to be able to know you I can count on and who I can trust because those are the people that are going to help me through things and make sure that you are there. When you are your best friend or having a good friend can make or break whatever you’re going through.
